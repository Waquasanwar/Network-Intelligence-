What we will not do

Worth stating plainly, because most of the industry does all of it.

  • ✕
    Send you a name without asking the person first

    Consent is per role and it is recorded. Withdraw it and the profile disappears from every client view immediately, including cards already on a shortlist.

  • ✕
    Scrape anyone into the network

    Everybody here had a conversation with us and chose to stay. Photos are optional and off by default — most profiles have none at all.

  • ✕
    Keep data forever

    Profiles and conversations are deleted 24 months after last contact. Fee and audit records we are legally required to keep are anonymised instead, and we say which.

  • ✕
    Send your words to a speech vendor

    Our interviewer can speak its questions aloud. What you say becomes text on your own device. No audio is uploaded or stored.

  • ✕
    Ask you for a nationality

    We record the right to work, which is the thing that affects whether somebody can take the job. Never the thing that does not.

  • ✕
    Charge you for a search

    No retainers, no exclusivity, no fee for a shortlist. If nobody starts, you pay nothing beyond the subscription.

Built to the stricter reading of both regimes that cover this network: UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 — the PDPL — which is consent-first and requires withdrawal to be as easy as consent was to give.
